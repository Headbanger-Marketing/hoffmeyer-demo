Three generations of the Hoffmeyer family, one shop on Kingston Street, since 1958.

Hoffmeyer Plumbing & Heating Limited was founded on May 2, 1958, and built by three generations of the Hoffmeyer family into the plumbing, heating, and cooling contractor that Goderich and Huron County call on. From a backed-up drain to a new furnace, a tankless water heater, or a gas fireplace, the work is done by licensed plumbers and gas-certified technicians who live in the community they serve.

In 2023 the company joined the Jayden's Mechanical family. The Hoffmeyer name, the Kingston Street location, and the crews all continue, now backed by a wider Southwestern Ontario group for parts, crews, and depth.

Since 1958

Three generations, one shop

The Hoffmeyer name has been on Kingston Street for more than sixty years. Here is how a plumbing shop founded in 1958 became the Huron County contractor it is today.

1958

Founded as Near & Hoffmeyer by Len Near and Jack Hoffmeyer at 59 Hamilton St in Goderich.

1960s

Jack Hoffmeyer buys out Len Near and moves the shop to Kingston Street.

1971

An Appliance Sales & Service division opens at 58 Kingston St.

1994

Jack retires and his son Doug Hoffmeyer takes the reins, the second generation.

2007

Doug's son Chris Hoffmeyer joins the business, the third generation.

2011

The Goderich tornado damages the building; the shop runs from 317 Huron Rd for about eighteen months before coming home.

2023

Hoffmeyer joins the Jayden's Mechanical family as its Goderich branch.

The namesake trade

Plumbing that keeps Goderich and Huron County running

Plumbing is the name on our door and the trade we have built since 1958. From a backed-up drain at midnight to a full new-construction rough-in, our licensed plumbers handle residential and commercial plumbing across Goderich and Huron County.

Explore plumbing

Plumbing is where the shop started, and it is still the backbone of the work. Drain and sewer cleaning, water heaters, repipes, fixtures, and new-construction rough-ins, with the jetting and camera equipment to diagnose underground problems without guessing.

From a midnight backup to a full reno rough-in, no job too big or too small.

The same crew that clears your drain can replace your water heater, repipe your home, and service the furnace and AC. One licensed shop, one point of accountability, three generations of doing it.

Questions

What Huron County asks us

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes, we are a licensed and insured plumbing, heating, and cooling contractor. We pull permits and arrange inspection on the work that requires it, every time.
Do you offer 24-hour emergency service?
Yes. A burst pipe, a no-heat call, or a sewer backup cannot always wait until morning. Call the 24-hour emergency line and we will get a technician out across Goderich and Huron County, day or night.
Are you gas-certified?
Yes, for both natural gas and liquid propane. We install and service furnaces, boilers, water heaters, fireplaces, and gas lines, and the fuel side is handled by the same licensed crew as the rest of the work.
Do you rent water heaters and equipment?
Yes. Through our Vista Credit program we offer rental and finance options on water heaters, furnaces, air conditioners, and boilers, including no-charge installation, monthly pre-authorized payment, and rate-increase protection. Exact terms are confirmed at the time of the quote.
What areas do you serve?
We are based at 55 Kingston St in Goderich and serve Goderich and the surrounding Huron County communities, including Clinton, Bayfield, Seaforth, and Blyth. If you are unsure whether we cover your address, call us and we will confirm.
Are you still the same Hoffmeyer from Goderich?
Yes. The Hoffmeyer name, the Kingston Street location, and the crews carry on under the Jayden's Mechanical family. Three generations of the Hoffmeyer family built this business since 1958, and for the customers who have called us for decades, the same standard of work continues, now backed by a wider Southwestern Ontario group.
